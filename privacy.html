Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

VARUN SAI BOOKSTORE (“we”) is a small independent bookstore for books by Varun Sai. Readers of every age are welcome to browse the books without creating an account. We collect as little personal information as we can, and this page explains what, why, and what you can do about it.

Legal status. India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 are being brought into force in phases. Most duties for organisations that handle personal data begin in May 2027. We are preparing for them now. This page is a plain-language notice and is not legal advice.

1. Who runs this site

This website is operated by Mrs. Thipparapu Sujatha on behalf of the author, Varun Sai. For privacy questions, requests and complaints, write to tsudhakartsudhakar5@gmail.com or use the data rights page.

2. Browsing without an account

You do not need an account or to give us any personal details to read about the books. When you open a page, your browser connects to services that make the site work, and they can see technical details such as your IP address, device and browser type:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Supabase holds our book information. The Books and book pages and the upcoming-books list ask Supabase for it whenever they load.
  • Google Fonts supplies the page fonts, so your browser contacts Google.
  • jsDelivr delivers a code library used by the Contact page.

We do not run advertising, analytics or tracking tools on this site, and we do not set cookies of our own. We do not track or profile anyone, including children.

3. What we collect, and why

Contact messages. If you use the Contact page we receive your name, email address and message. We use them only to read your message and reply to you. They are kept in our Supabase database and are meant to be seen only by the bookstore’s authorised administrators.

Reader accounts (adults only). To write a review you can create an account with your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by Supabase’s sign-in service and are not readable by us. At sign-up we also record that you declared you are 18 or older and which version of this notice you saw. Your browser keeps a sign-in token in its local storage so you stay signed in; signing out removes it.

Reviews. We store your star rating, your review text and a link to your account. After the author approves a review, the website shows its rating, text and any author reply as “Reader”. Your email address and account details are not shown. Anything you write in a review becomes public, so please do not put phone numbers, addresses or other people’s details in it. We may hide or remove reviews that break our Terms of Use.

What we do not collect. We do not take payments on this website, so we do not collect card or UPI details. If that changes we will update this page first.

4. Children

Children are welcome to browse the public pages of this bookstore without registering.

Reader accounts, reviews and the Contact form are for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly accept personal data from children, and we have not yet set up the verified parental-consent process that the law will require before we could. A tick-box on a website cannot prove anyone’s age, so it is only a reminder and a declaration. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to write to us for you. If you think a child has given us personal data, email tsudhakartsudhakar5@gmail.com and we will delete it.

5. Who else handles your data

We share personal data only with the service providers above (Supabase and GitHub for hosting and storage) so the site can work. We do not sell or rent your information. We may disclose information if a court or authority legally requires it. Some providers may store data on servers outside India. Amazon and Pothi are separate companies: when you tap a buy button you leave this site and their own privacy policies apply.

6. How long we keep it

  • Contact messages: until your question is dealt with, then up to 12 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Reader accounts: until you ask us to delete them, or after 24 months without signing in.
  • Reviews: while the book is listed, or until you ask us to remove them. When an account is deleted we delete its reviews too.
  • The law may require certain records or technical logs to be kept for a minimum time, for example a one-year minimum under the DPDP Rules for some logs. Those are kept only as long as required.

7. Keeping data safe

We use secure (HTTPS) connections, a sign-in service run by Supabase, and database access rules and an administrator check to limit who can read or change data. We keep secret keys out of the public website files. We are a small operation, our checks are limited, and no online service can promise complete security. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a summary of the personal data we hold about you; to correct, complete or update it; to erase it; or to withdraw a consent you gave. You can also nominate another person to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act for yourself. Use the data rights page or email tsudhakartsudhakar5@gmail.com from the address you used with us, so we can check it is you. We will reply as quickly as we can and always within 90 days, which is the longest time the DPDP Rules allow for a grievance.

9. Complaints

Please raise any concern with us first at tsudhakartsudhakar5@gmail.com. If we do not resolve it, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is receiving complaints; the Act expects you to try our grievance process first.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. This policy was last reviewed on 13 November 2026 and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.
