Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

VARUN SAI BOOKSTORE ("we") is a small independent bookstore for books by Varun Sai. We collect as little information as possible. This page explains what, why, and your choices.

Who runs this site

This website is operated by Thipparapu Sujatha on behalf of the author, Varun Sai. You can reach us through the Contact page.

What we collect

Contact messages. If you use the Contact page, we receive your name, email address and message.

Reader accounts and reviews. If you create an account to review a book, we store your email address and a password (kept in scrambled form by our login provider, never readable by us). If you post a review, we store its rating and text. Reviews are shown publicly as "Reader", without your name or email. We may remove reviews that are abusive or inappropriate.

What we do not collect. We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics tools, and we do not take payments on this website yet. If that changes, this page will be updated first.

How we use it

Messages are used only to read and reply to you. Account details are used only to sign you in and attach your review to your account. We do not sell or rent your information.

Who else handles data

Supabase stores our database and logins. GitHub Pages hosts the website. Google Fonts supplies the page fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads. Amazon and Pothi are separate companies: when you click a buy button you leave this site and their own privacy policies apply.

Browser storage

If you sign in as a reader, your browser keeps a sign-in token so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it. We do not use advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and accounts only as long as needed, and delete them when you ask.

Your choices

You can ask us, through the Contact page, to delete your account, your messages or your reviews, or to correct your details.

Children

Reader accounts and reviews are for people aged 18 and over. Younger readers are welcome to browse the books but should not create an account or send personal details.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.

This page is a plain-language summary and is not legal advice.
