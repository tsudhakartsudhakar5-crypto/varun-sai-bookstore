# VARUN SAI BOOKSTORE
## Privacy Policy — Draft for Review

**Version:** 1.0  
**Prepared:** 11 November 2026  
**Website:** https://tsudhakartsudhakar5-crypto.github.io/varun-sai-bookstore/

### 1. Introduction
This policy explains how information may be handled when visitors browse the VARUN SAI BOOKSTORE website or contact its operator. The bookstore provides information about books and may link visitors to external publishing or retail platforms.

### 2. Browsing and accounts
Visitors can browse public bookstore pages without registering or signing in. No customer account-registration feature is confirmed active in the reviewed project context.

### 3. Contact information
When a visitor submits the contact form, the website may collect:
- name;
- email address; and
- message/body.

The current source code submits these fields to the Supabase `messages` table. Visitors should not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or sensitive personal information through the contact form.

### 4. Purposes
Contact information may be used to:
- respond to questions and support requests;
- understand and resolve bookstore enquiries;
- maintain records needed to handle a request;
- protect the website against spam or misuse; and
- meet applicable legal obligations.

### 5. Technical information
The hosting platform, backend provider and browser may process technical information such as request metadata, timestamps, device/browser information and security logs. The exact categories and retention periods must be confirmed from provider settings and current integrations.

### 6. Payments, orders and downloads
The current evidence does not establish that the website itself processes payments, operates a live checkout, stores payment-card details or provides secure direct ebook downloads. The site has used external retailer/publisher links. If these features are later introduced, this policy must be revised before launch to identify the data collected, payment provider, order records, delivery process, retention and refund/support arrangements.

### 7. Sharing and service providers
Information may be processed by providers necessary to host and operate the website, including GitHub Pages and Supabase. External retailer links take visitors to third-party websites governed by their own privacy policies. Information is not intended to be sold.

### 8. Retention
Contact messages should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the enquiry and meet legitimate legal/security needs. Proposed internal review periods are in the Operations Manual. Actual live database backups, logs and provider retention periods have not yet been verified.

### 9. Security and access
The website uses a backend database. Access to private contact records should be limited to authorised administrators through verified access controls. The bookstore's current RLS policies, table grants, function definitions, storage settings and live access restrictions have not yet been fully audited in this package. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

### 10. Children's privacy
The website may be accessible to children. Public browsing should not require an account. Avoid unnecessary collection of children's personal data. Any future account, payment, targeted advertising or child-directed feature requires a separate legal and safeguarding review and appropriate parent/guardian involvement.

### 11. Requests and grievances
For privacy questions, correction/deletion requests or grievances, contact:

**Email:** tsudhakartsudhakar5@gmail.com

Please provide enough information to locate the relevant enquiry without sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Requests will be reviewed and handled as reasonably practicable, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

### 12. External websites
Links to retailers, publishers or other external services are provided for convenience. Their own terms and privacy policies apply once visitors leave this website.

### 13. Policy changes
This policy may be updated when the website's features, providers or applicable requirements change. Update the version and effective date whenever a new version is published.
