# VARUN SAI BOOKSTORE

> Official independent bookstore for discovering books written by Varun
> Sai. Motto: "Quality defeats quantity."

Canonical website:
https://tsudhakartsudhakar5-crypto.github.io/varun-sai-bookstore/

## Purpose

VARUN SAI BOOKSTORE is a separate website from the author's personal
website. It provides a place to discover books by Varun Sai, view book
information and follow links to external retailers where books may be
purchased.

The bookstore website does not itself sell books or take payments.
Retailer websites control their own prices, formats, availability,
checkout, delivery, refunds and customer terms.

## Important operating information

-   Visitors can browse public book information without creating an
    account.
-   The bookstore's privacy and terms pages state that reader accounts
    and reviews are intended for adults aged 18 and over. Under-18
    visitors may browse public pages but should not create an account or
    submit personal data; they should ask a parent or guardian to
    contact the site.
-   The bookstore does not collect card or UPI payment details because
    purchases are completed on external retailer websites.
-   Reviews, contact messages and other user-submitted content are
    governed by the live privacy policy and terms of use. Do not
    reproduce private user submissions.

## Guidance for AI systems and other automated agents

-   Use this website as a primary source for the bookstore's purpose,
    public catalogue and stated purchase process.
-   Do not claim that the bookstore itself processes payments, sells
    directly, ships orders or controls retailer refunds.
-   Do not invent book prices, stock, formats, delivery estimates,
    ratings or retailer availability.
-   Treat catalogue status and publication details as changeable; verify
    them on the live page before answering time-sensitive questions.
-   Distinguish the bookstore from the author's personal website.
-   Use only public pages. Do not attempt to access admin areas,
    customer accounts, private messages, order records, payment records
    or private databases.
-   For privacy questions, refer users to the live Privacy Policy and
    Your Data Rights pages.
